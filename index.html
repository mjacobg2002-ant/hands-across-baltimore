Psychiatric Rehabilitation Program

Practical support beyond the therapy room.

Psychiatric rehabilitation helps eligible adults and young people strengthen daily routines, coping strategies, independence, communication, and community connection — building on individual strengths and goals.

Adult PRP

For adults ages 18 and older

Individualized support outside of traditional therapy for adults who may benefit from help with daily living, coping, and community integration.

Child & Adolescent PRP

For youth ages 5–17

One-on-one support coordinated where appropriate with families, schools, therapists, and community supports.

Substance Use Treatment

Recovery support without judgment.

Outpatient support that meets people with compassion — including assessments, individual counseling, group services, peer recovery support, harm-reduction education, and coordination for co-occurring mental-health needs.

Explore Substance Use Treatment
Medication Management

Thoughtful, collaborative medication support.

Board-certified psychiatric providers offer comprehensive psychiatric evaluations, medication support tailored to individual needs, and regular follow-up to monitor effectiveness — coordinated with therapists, primary care, and PRP counselors.
